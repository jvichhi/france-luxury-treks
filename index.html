Complete Trip Checklist

Documents & Money

  • Canadian passport valid 6+ months past return date; no visa needed for stays under 90 days (Schengen)
  • Travel insurance with medical evacuation coverage — essential for the Mont Blanc and Pyrenees options given remote terrain
  • Currency: Euro throughout France, Italy, and (soon) most of the route; Swiss Francs if overnighting on the Swiss side of a Mont Blanc variant
  • Notify your bank/credit card of European travel dates

Connectivity

  • EU-wide eSIM (e.g. Airalo, Holafly) is simpler than roaming add-ons, especially for the Mont Blanc route crossing 3 countries
  • Offline maps downloaded in advance (Komoot, Maps.me) as rural wifi is unreliable

Packing Essentials

  • Broken-in hiking boots (not new) — the single biggest factor in trip comfort
  • Layered clothing: alpine and mountain routes swing from single digits °C at dawn to 25°C+ midday
  • Rain shell and trekking poles, especially for Mont Blanc, Pyrenees, and Brittany's exposed coastal sections
  • Power adapter: Type E/F for France (also covers most of Italy); Type J needed only if overnighting in Switzerland
  • Sun protection and a lightweight sleeping bag liner for any refuge/basic guesthouse nights

Booking Timeline

  • Flights: book 5–7 months ahead for the best summer economy fares from Montreal
  • Luxury/spa hotel rooms: 9–12 months ahead, especially July–August
  • Restaurant reservations (Michelin-starred tables): 4–8 weeks ahead minimum
