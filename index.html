For guaranteed spa access every single night with zero logistics headache, the Chamonix Basecamp (DIY) option is now the strongest match: one 5-star wellness residence for the whole trip, no guide required for 5 of 6 hiking days, and roughly half the cost of the guided version of the same concept. If you specifically want to say you hiked the actual TMB circuit through 3 countries, the Mont Blanc Half-Circuit remains the only option that does that, at the cost of 2 unavoidable refuge nights. The Luberon (revised) is the strongest fully-French, moving-itinerary pick with genuine 5-star spa properties at Le Phébus and Capelongue.
